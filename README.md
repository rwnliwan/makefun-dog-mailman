## The entry

> Restraining order filed. He came anyway. Five stars for the commitment.

71 characters. The dog escalates to the law, the mailman ignores the order
and comes anyway, and the dog's verdict flips from punishment to praise:
the one human who will not be scared off earns five stars for the
commitment.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. No secrets,
no network, no dependencies.
